## What I believe, and the receipts

A demo convinces you. Users convince everyone else.

→ My **[hackathon issue desk](https://sihsupport.vercel.app)** ran a live event: 278 teams, 1,600+ participants, and no second take.

### 2 · Safe by default.

A power tool should be hard to misuse.

→ vazr shows you everything it found, sorted by category, before it touches a single file.

### 3 · Make the model show its work.

An AI answer with no evidence is a guess with good grammar.

→ **FraudSense** scores a case, retrieves the evidence, and hands the final call to a human.<br/>
→ **[Twinance](https://twinance.vercel.app)**'s co-pilot answers from your own recent transactions, so you can check it.

### 4 · When code touches the physical world, boring is a feature.

→ **Veridose** is a pill dispenser: ESP32 firmware, MQTT, a backend and a phone app. It doesn't get to "move fast and break things."<br/>
→ **[Solarnexa](https://solarnexa.org)**: solar trees with EV charging for dense cities. Early, and still being built.

### 5 · Build the room, not just the thing.

→ As president of [GitHub Community GITAM](https://github.com/github-community-gitam) I led a 28-person team and ran workshops and a 2,500-student tech fest. We won Best Technical Club.
